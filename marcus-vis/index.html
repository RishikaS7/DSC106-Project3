What this chart tells us

This graph compares winter temperatures in six places with different climates. Under SSP5-8.5 all six warm in this simulation, but London, Berlin and Sapporo already have positive 20-winter means in 2014, while Beijing, Minneapolis and Montreal remain below zero in that mean through 2100. The lines describe nearby model cells, not city observations.

What this chart tells us

Every location shows less late-century warming under lower emissions in this simulation. Montreal warms by about 2.5°C under SSP1-2.6 compared with 6.9°C under SSP5-8.5. The gap compares two possible pathways; it does not quantify uncertainty across climate models or guarantee a specific outcome.

Reading the data carefully

Winter = December–February, weighted by calendar month length. December belongs to the following winter year. Winter 2015 combines historical December 2014 with scenario January–February 2015. A trailing mean is labeled at its final year: 2100 represents 2081–2100. Early projected means include historical winters.

The freezing reference is 0°C / 32°F. An average above freezing does not mean freezing days, frost or snow disappear. No future first upward crossing of the trailing 20-winter mean occurs in these six cells during 2015–2100.

Nearest native grid cells, no bias correction or land masking. Sapporo's coarse coastal cell may poorly represent the city. One model and member cannot quantify model uncertainty. Historical values are shared across scenarios and are drawn once.
