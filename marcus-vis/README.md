## Different Climates, a Shared Warming Threat

This graph compares winter temperatures in six places with different climates across North America, Europe, and East Asia under the very-high-emissions scenario, SSP5-8.5. The lines show trailing 20-winter averages, and the dashed line marks freezing at 0°C/32°F. All six places warm in this simulation, but London, Berlin, and Sapporo already average above freezing when projections begin, while Beijing, Minneapolis, and Montreal remain below it through 2100. These temperatures represent nearby model grid cells, not city observations.

## Different Emissions, Different Winter Futures

This graph compares how much those same places warm under lower versus very high emissions. Each dot shows the change from the 1995–2014 winter average to the 2081–2100 average, rather than the actual temperature. All six places show less warming under lower emissions in this simulation. For example, Montreal warms by about 2.5°C under SSP1-2.6 compared with 6.9°C under SSP5-8.5, showing how emissions choices can affect future winter warming.
